# What's new in Umm

This file is the "What's new" page in the app and the top of each release's notes. Write for the people who use
Umm: what changed for them, in plain words. One `## version` heading per release, newest first, with an optional
date after a dash, then one `- ` line per change. A line may start with a short `**bold title.**`.

## 1.3.0

- **Umm for Linux.** A desktop app, tested on KDE Plasma and GNOME: press a hotkey, speak, and the text is typed into the window you are in. It sits in the tray, can start at login, and has the same settings, history and stats pages as the phone app.
- **Linux packages.** Install from a tarball, a .deb, an .rpm or a Flatpak.
- **Sync between your devices.** Pair a phone and a computer with a code or a QR scan. History, stats, settings and app categories then stay the same on both. Devices sync directly over Wi-Fi when they can reach each other, and through encrypted relay messages when they cannot. The relays cannot read anything, and there is no Umm account or server.
- **Spoken emoji.** Say "thumbs up emoji" and get 👍.
- **What's new.** This page, under Settings.
- **Newer Android devices.** Umm now runs on devices that use 16 KB memory pages.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-09-30

- **Floating button.** Dictate into any app from a small button on the screen: tap it to start, or hold it to talk. Drag it to an edge to dock it.
- **Stats dashboard.** Charts of your dictation habits, productivity and cost, on cards you can rearrange.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-09-27

- **First release.** A voice keyboard for Android that uses your own OpenRouter key: speak, and Umm types the cleaned-up text.
- **Cleanup levels.** Choose how much Umm tidies what you say, per app category.
- **History and models.** Look back at past dictations, and pick the speech and cleanup models yourself.
- **Zero data retention.** Limit Umm to providers that do not keep your audio or text.
